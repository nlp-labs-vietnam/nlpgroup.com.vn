Grasen T480Q 480kW 4-Gun DC EV Fast Charger for High-Traffic Charging Sites

Grasen has launched the T480Q, a new 480kW 4-gun DC fast charger designed for high-throughput commercial charging environments such as highway service areas, large fleet depots, and public fast charging hubs.

Product Overview: T480Q

The T480Q expands Grasen's Thunder series with a four-connector configuration in a single unit, enabling simultaneous charging of up to four vehicles from a single power cabinet.

Key Specifications:
- Maximum output power: 480 kW
- Number of connectors: 4 guns (dual-side, 2 per side)
- Per-gun output: Up to 240 kW (dynamic power sharing between connected vehicles)
- Output voltage range: 150V–1,000V DC
- Supported protocols: OCPP 1.6J
- Display: 21.5-inch LCD touchscreen
- Authentication: RFID, QR code, mobile app, Plug & Charge
- Connector options: CCS2, CHAdeMO, GB/T, NACS (configurable per market)
- IP rating: IP54
- Operating temperature: -30°C to +55°C
- Warranty: 26 months

Dynamic Power Sharing

The T480Q uses dynamic power distribution — total available power (up to 480kW) is allocated across active charging sessions based on each vehicle's demand:
- If one vehicle is charging at full 240kW demand, remaining 240kW is available for other guns
- As additional vehicles connect, power is redistributed proportionally
- When a vehicle's battery reaches near-full and charging rate drops, freed capacity is reallocated to other active sessions

This ensures maximum hardware utilization and minimizes per-session wait time at high-traffic sites.

Design for High-Traffic Commercial Sites

The T480Q addresses specific challenges at busy charging locations:
- High throughput: Up to 4 simultaneous sessions vs 1–2 guns on typical DC chargers
- Reduced footprint: 4 guns from a single cabinet vs 4 separate standalone units
- Flexible connector mix: Different connector types on different guns to serve mixed fleets
- Serviceability: Modular power modules can be swapped without full unit replacement

Site Applications:
- Highway service areas: Serve multiple vehicles during short stops (20–40 min sessions)
- Fleet depot rapid charging: Shift-change fast charging for commercial vehicle fleets
- Destination charging (airports, stadiums): High volume with shorter sessions
- Public fast charging hubs: Urban EV charging corridors

Connectivity and Software

The T480Q integrates with Grasen's CSMS (Charging Station Management System) via OCPP 1.6J:
- Remote monitoring and fault diagnostics
- Per-gun session management and billing
- Dynamic pricing configuration
- Firmware update over the air
- Multi-tenant operator support

Physical Dimensions and Installation

- Footprint: Compact single-cabinet design
- Cable management: Integrated overhead cable routing system
- Power input: Three-phase AC, 480V or 400V depending on market variant
- Installation: Requires dedicated transformer for full 480kW output at single location
- Mounting: Floor-mounted, weather-resistant outdoor enclosure

Availability and Markets

The T480Q is available for international B2B orders. Connector configuration (CCS1 for North America, CCS2 for Europe/Asia, GB/T for China, NACS for select markets) is specified at order. Lead time is 6–12 weeks depending on volume and destination.

Conclusion

The Grasen T480Q 480kW 4-gun DC fast charger addresses the growing need for high-throughput charging at busy commercial sites. By combining four charging connections in a single power cabinet with dynamic power distribution, it offers site operators higher vehicle throughput, lower footprint, and reduced installation cost compared to multiple standalone units.
