Invitation: Meet GRASEN at the 140th Canton Fair 2026

GRASEN cordially invites partners, distributors, and EV charging project developers to visit our booth at the 140th Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair), October 15–19, 2026, in Guangzhou, China.

Event Details

Event: The 140th Canton Fair (China Import and Export Fair) — Phase 3
Dates: October 15–19, 2026
Location: China Import and Export Fair Complex, Guangzhou, China
GRASEN Booth: Hall 8.1, Booth G20

About the Canton Fair

The Canton Fair is the world's largest trade fair, held twice annually in Guangzhou. With over 200,000 buyers from 200+ countries attending each edition, it is the premier platform for global sourcing in electronics, industrial equipment, and clean energy.

Phase 3 of the 140th edition covers energy and environmental protection equipment, including EV charging infrastructure — making it the ideal venue for GRASEN to present its latest product innovations to international buyers.

What GRASEN Will Exhibit

1. T480Q — New 480kW Four-Gun DC Fast Charger
GRASEN's latest high-power flagship: 480kW output across 4 guns with dynamic power distribution. Designed for highway service areas and high-traffic commercial charging sites.

2. R Series DC Chargers
The new R 40 and R 240 models: wallbox-format and standalone DC fast chargers for medium-traffic commercial applications. Compact design, IP54-rated, OCPP 1.6J compliant.

3. AC Charger Lineup
Full range: Genie (7–22kW), Pedestal (7–22kW), Mini Smart (7–22kW), Smart AC Charger (7/22kW). Suitable for residential, workplace, and hospitality installations.

4. EV Charging Solutions & CSMS Demo
Live demonstration of Grasen's Charging Station Management System (CSMS) — real-time monitoring, user management, dynamic pricing, and reporting via the Grasen operator portal and mobile app.

Why Visit GRASEN at Canton Fair

- Direct access to GRASEN's international sales and technical teams
- Sample products on display for hands-on inspection
- Custom project consultation — tailored solutions for your market
- Discussion of OEM/white-label partnerships
- Special pricing packages for orders placed during the fair
