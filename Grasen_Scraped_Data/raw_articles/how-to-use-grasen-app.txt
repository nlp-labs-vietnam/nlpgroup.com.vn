How to Use the Grasen App for EV Charging and Payment

The Grasen App is the mobile companion for Grasen-networked EV charging stations. It allows EV drivers to find nearby chargers, check real-time availability, initiate and monitor charging sessions, and manage payments — all from a smartphone.

Downloading and Setting Up the App

The Grasen App is available on:
- Apple App Store (iOS 13 and later)
- Google Play Store (Android 8.0 and later)

After downloading:
1. Open the app and tap "Register"
2. Enter your email address and create a password
3. Verify your email via the confirmation link
4. Add a payment method (credit/debit card or supported mobile payment)
5. Optionally link your EV for personalized charging recommendations

Finding Nearby Charging Stations

The app's map view shows all Grasen-networked stations in your area:
- Filter by charger type (AC/DC), power level, and connector type
- View real-time availability: green = available, red = occupied, grey = offline
- Tap a station for details: address, operating hours, pricing, connector types
- Navigate directly to the station via integrated map directions

Starting a Charging Session

Method 1 — QR Code Scan:
1. Arrive at the charging station
2. Open the app and tap "Scan QR Code"
3. Point the camera at the QR code on the charger
4. Select the connector and confirm pricing
5. Plug in your vehicle and tap "Start Charging"

Method 2 — RFID Card:
Register your RFID card in the app under "My Account → Cards". Tap the card on the reader to start a session. The session is linked to your app account for billing.

Method 3 — Remote Start (if pre-authorized):
For fleet operators with pre-authorized vehicles, sessions can be initiated remotely via the app or operator dashboard.

Monitoring Your Charging Session

During an active session, the app shows:
- Current charging power (kW)
- Energy delivered (kWh)
- Time elapsed and estimated completion
- Current cost
- Battery level (for compatible vehicles via OCPP)

You'll receive push notifications when:
- The session starts (confirming connection)
- Charging completes
- The idle/overtime timer begins

Stopping a Charging Session

1. Open the app and go to "Active Session"
2. Tap "Stop Charging"
3. Unplug the cable after the session ends
4. Payment is processed automatically

Payment and Invoices

- Payments are deducted automatically from your registered payment method
- Session receipts are available in "Charging History → View Invoice"
- Monthly statements are available for fleet accounts
- Prepaid wallet balance can be topped up in the app

Reviewing Your Charging Records

The "Charging History" section shows:
- Date, time, and location of each session
- Energy consumed (kWh), duration, and cost
- Charger details and session ID (for dispute resolution)
- Export to CSV for expense reporting

Fleet and Business Features

Fleet managers can access the web portal for:
- Multi-driver account management
- Bulk RFID card management
- Cost center allocation and monthly billing reports
- API integration with fleet management software

Troubleshooting

- Charger shows as offline: Check the app for alternative nearby stations; contact support if persistent
- Payment failed: Verify card details under "My Account → Payment Methods"
- Session won't start: Ensure the cable is fully connected; try restarting the app

Support

In-app chat and a help center with FAQs are accessible under "Menu → Help & Support". Email and WhatsApp support are also available via the Grasen website.
