Top 10 DC Fast Charger Manufacturers in China (2026): Global Buyer's Guide

China is the world's largest EV market and home to a substantial portion of global DC fast charger manufacturing capacity. For international buyers, understanding the competitive landscape helps in selecting the right supplier for specific project requirements.

1. GRASEN (Luoyang Jiasheng New Energy)
Strengths: Full product range from 30kW wallbox to 1,600kW distributed systems; OCPP compliant; 26-month warranty; strong global distribution network across 90+ countries
Product focus: DC fast chargers (T, R, S series), AC chargers, CSMS software
Best for: Commercial CPO deployments, fleet depots, international B2B buyers

2. Star Charge (Wanbang New Energy)

Product focus: AC and DC chargers; integrated charging solutions

How to Evaluate DC Fast Charger Manufacturers

Key criteria for international B2B buyers:
1. Certifications: CE, UL, CB, RoHS, IP rating, OCPP compliance
2. Warranty: 24+ months on hardware is standard for tier-1 suppliers
3. Global support: Service network, spare parts availability, remote diagnostics
4. Connector options: CCS1/2, CHAdeMO, NACS, GB/T — configure to target market
5. CSMS integration: Open OCPP vs proprietary platform lock-in
6. Production capacity: Ability to fulfill large orders within committed lead times
7. References: Deployed project evidence in target country/region

Conclusion

The DC fast charger market in China has matured significantly, with multiple manufacturers capable of supplying international-grade products.
